Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 23, 2026

Thank you for choosing Zsurf Browser. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains how we handle information in our browser, specifically regarding our assistive tools, AI features, and third-party integrations.

🔒 Core Privacy Commitment

We DO NOT collect, store, or sell your personal browsing history, search queries, or private data. Your browsing activity remains strictly on your device.

1. Advanced Interaction & Assistive Features (Gesture Control)

Zsurf Browser includes an innovative Hand Gesture Control (Virtual Mouse) feature.

Accessibility Service API Usage: To provide a hands-free experience, this feature utilizes the Android Accessibility Service API. This allows the app to perform system-wide clicks and cursor movements on behalf of the user.

Purpose: This is primarily designed as an assistive tool for users with motor impairments or those who cannot interact with the touch screen.

Data Privacy: This service works entirely on-device. It DO NOT capture images, record videos, or store any data related to your hand landmarks or gestures. No data from the camera or accessibility service is ever transmitted to us or any third party.

2. AI & Smart Features

We offer several AI-powered tools to enhance your productivity:

  • AI Chat & Smart Translate: These features help you summarize content and break language barriers. All interactions are designed with a "privacy-first" approach.
  • Voice Control Browser: This functionality uses microphone access only when activated by the user to perform browser commands. Voice data is processed securely and is not stored or analyzed for profiling.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Browsing History: We do not track the websites you visit. History is stored locally on your device and can be cleared at any time.
  • Search Queries: We do not log your search terms.
  • Personal Identifiers: We do not collect names, phone numbers, or email addresses unless you voluntarily contact support.

4. Third-Party Services & Integrations

Zsurf Browser provides access to third-party services which are subject to their own privacy policies.

Search Engines: Choose from Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo, and Exa Search (AI Search). While we do not store your queries, these engines may have their own data practices.

Entertainment & News Hubs: When you interact with platforms like Odysee, Rumble, Dailymotion, BBC News, or Al Jazeera within our browser, you are interacting directly with those sites.

5. Local Data Storage

To improve your experience, some data is stored only locally on your device:

  • Cookies & Cache: Used by websites to keep you logged in.
  • Bookmarks & Shortcuts: Your saved sites are stored only on your device.
  • Tab State: Open tabs are saved locally for session resumption.

6. Permissions We Request

The app requires the following permissions to function:

  • Internet Access: Required to browse the web.
  • Camera: Required only for the Hand Gesture Control feature (Processed locally).
  • Microphone: Required only for Voice Control/Browsing.
  • Storage (Optional): Required only if you download files from the web.
  • Accessibility Service: Required only if you enable Gesture Control (Assistive tool).

7. Smart Security Features

We implement built-in security features to protect you:

  • Popup Blocker: Prevents unwanted windows from opening.
  • Do Not Track: Sends requests to websites not to track your activity.
  • Incognito Mode: Ensures no history or cookies are saved after the session ends.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as we add new features. Changes will be posted here with an updated "Last Updated" date.
